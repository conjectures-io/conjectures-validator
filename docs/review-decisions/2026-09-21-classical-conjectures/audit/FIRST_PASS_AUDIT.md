## Specific holds and corrections

- **#8, #19, #25:** selected main statements look standard, but surrounding “research solved” claims rest on a submitted Ferreira preprint. The arXiv entry reports “submitted to Annals of Mathematics”; this review did not establish community acceptance. A submitted manuscript alone is insufficient to certify the status tag.
- **#17:** the formal proposition says the three known n values are the complete list. Its surrounding prose asks whether there are additional solutions. These questions have opposite Boolean answers; retain the correct proposition-to-UI mapping.
- **#18, #23:** historical attribution/scope qualifications are necessary. These are not verbatim ancient/original formulations.
- **#32, #34:** analytic custom definitions merit further semantic and elaborated-type checks before admission.
- **#38:** only four-dimensional Borsuk is an open candidate; the unrestricted conjecture is false.
