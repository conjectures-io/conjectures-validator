## What the informal problem asks

Erdős Problem 15 asks whether

\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{n}{p_n}
\]

converges, where \(p_n\) is the \(n\)-th prime. This is the ordinary real-valued convergence
question stated by [erdosproblems.com](https://www.erdosproblems.com/15).

## What the published Lean task asks

At the pinned source revision, the decisive part of `Erdos15.erdos_15` is:

```lean
Summable (fun k : ℕ =>
  (-1 : ℚ) ^ (k + 1) * (k + 1) / (k.nth Nat.Prime))
```

The explicit `ℚ` makes this a series in the rational numbers. Lean's `Summable` predicate therefore
requires its partial sums to converge to a limit in `ℚ`.

That is not equivalent to the informal real-convergence question. The rational numbers are not
complete: a sequence of rational partial sums may converge to an irrational real number while
having no limit in `ℚ`. Consequently, failure of `ℚ`-summability can mean either:

1. the series does not converge over the reals; or
2. the series converges over the reals but its sum is irrational.

Only the first possibility would answer the informal problem negatively. The published Lean target
does not distinguish between them.

The source history confirms that this difference was not part of the original mathematical
question. The initial pull-request version explicitly quantified a limit `m : ℝ`; during review it
was refactored to `Summable` and the coefficient type was changed to `ℚ`. See the
[original real-valued draft](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/pull/3771#discussion_r3094795709)
and the
[follow-up recording the change to rationals](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/pull/3771#discussion_r3098656848).

## Why this is a formalization defect

Changing the ambient number system changes the mathematical proposition. Here it turns a question
about the existence of a real limit into the stronger demand that the limit exist as a rational
number. The counterexample succeeds against that stronger published statement without settling
the intended conjecture.

The miner did not weaken, replace, or alter the challenge. The mismatch was present in the task we
published. Under the manual reward-review policy, responsibility for that mismatch belongs to the
validator, so `TRIVIALISED_STATEMENT` does not apply.
